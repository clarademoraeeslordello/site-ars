## 1. What they are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar feature. This
policy explains which ones we use on the platform (`app.auditcockpits.com`) and on the website
(`auditcockpits.com`).

## 2. Platform (`app.auditcockpits.com`)

We only use what is **necessary for the service you asked for**: signing in securely and keeping the
preferences you chose. These items therefore do not require consent.

| Name / type | Purpose | Duration | Party |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sign-in session cookies (Auth.js) | Keep you securely signed in | Up to 30 days in the browser; access expires earlier, when the identity provider session ends (8 hours of inactivity or 24 hours at most) | First party |
| Identity provider cookies (`auth.auditcockpits.com`) | Single sign-on session and protection of the sign-in flow | Until the sign-in session ends | First party |
| `acting_org_id` | Remember which company a platform administrator is working in | Browser session | First party |
| `ars_locale`, `NEXT_LOCALE` | Remember the language you chose | Up to 1 year | First party |
| Local storage: language and theme choice | Remember language and light/dark theme | Until you clear your browser | First party |

We do not use advertising, social media or third-party analytics cookies on the platform.

## 3. Website (`auditcockpits.com`)

| Name / type | Purpose | Duration | Consent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local storage `ars-analytics-consent` | Remember your choice about analytics cookies | Until you clear your browser | Not required (records the choice) |
| `ars_admin` | Staff session in the website publishing area (entered through a single-use link valid for 15 minutes) | 7 days | Not required (staff only) |
| Google Analytics 4 (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) | Visit statistics, with IP anonymisation | Up to 2 years | **Required** — currently **off** |

The website currently **loads no analytics tools**. If Google Analytics is switched on, nothing will load
before you choose, refusing will be as easy as accepting, you can change your choice from the footer, and
Google will be added to the subprocessor list.

## 4. How to control them

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. On the platform this signs you out; on the
website it clears your choice, which will be asked again.
