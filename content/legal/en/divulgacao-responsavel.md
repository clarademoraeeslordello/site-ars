## 1. Channel
Send reports to **`dpo@auditcockpits.com`**, with a description, steps to reproduce, estimated impact and how to
reach you. We accept reports in Portuguese, English or Spanish.

## 2. Scope
- `auditcockpits.com`
- `app.auditcockpits.com`
- `auth.auditcockpits.com`

## 3. Out of scope
- Denial-of-service attacks (DoS/DDoS) or load tests;
- social engineering, phishing or contacting staff and customers;
- physical attacks on offices or data centres;
- mass automated scans and tool-only reports without proof of impact;
- third-party services outside our control.

## 4. Rules
1. **Do not access, change or delete other people's data.** Use only your own or test accounts.
2. If you find another person's or organisation's data, **stop immediately** and report it, without keeping a copy.
3. Do not degrade the service or try to keep persistent access.
4. **Do not disclose** the vulnerability before it is fixed or agreed with us.
5. We will acknowledge receipt within **5 business days**, keep you updated on the analysis and aim to fix or mitigate within **7 days** (critical), **30 days** (high) and **90 days** (medium), and in the next update (low).

## 5. Safe harbour
Research carried out in good faith and in line with this policy is considered authorised: we will not take legal
action against you for it and, if third parties do, we will make it clear that the activity was authorised.

## 6. Rewards
There is currently **no bug bounty programme**. With your permission, we will publicly acknowledge your
contribution.

## 7. security.txt
Published at `https://auditcockpits.com/.well-known/security.txt` and `https://app.auditcockpits.com/.well-known/security.txt` (RFC 9116).
