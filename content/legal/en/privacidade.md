## 1. Who we are

This Policy explains how **Audit Cockpits** ("we"), operated by **.NETT TECNOLOGIA LTDA**, Brazilian company
registration (CNPJ) **62.056.161/0001-54**, registered office at **Rua Pais Leme, 215, suite 1713, Pinheiros, São Paulo/SP, CEP 05424-150, Brazil**, processes personal data on the website
`auditcockpits.com`, on the platform `app.auditcockpits.com` and in related services.

Audit Cockpits is a SaaS platform that helps companies prepare for audits and certifications (such as ISO
27001, ISO 9001 and the LGPD) through guided journeys, controls, activities, evidence and readiness
indicators (Audit Readiness Score).

We follow the **Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 — LGPD)** and, where it applies
to customers or users in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the **General Data Protection
Regulation (GDPR)** and the **UK GDPR**. Annex A adds information for the EEA and the UK; Annex B, for
residents of the United States.

## 2. Our role in each processing activity

| Situation | Our role | Who decides about the data |
|---|---|---|
| User account, sign-in, security, support, contracts and billing | **Controller** | Audit Cockpits |
| Content entered by the customer on the platform (evidence, documents, audit records, employee data, the customer's own LGPD inventory) | **Processor** | The customer company (controller) |
| Corporate website, demo requests and newsletter | **Controller** | Audit Cockpits |

When we act as **processor**, we process data only on the customer's instructions and under the contract.
Requests from individuals about that data are forwarded to the customer, who decides on them.

**Partner consultancies.** Some customers buy Audit Cockpits through a partner consultancy. In that case,
the partner's consultants may access the customer's organisation **only with an active authorisation**,
with a limited role and an expiry date, which the customer's administrator can revoke at any time. Every
action by a consultant is recorded in the audit trail, showing that the access came from the partner. In
the *white label* model, the platform may show the consultancy's name and brand; Audit Cockpits remains the
technology provider and the processor of the data.

## 5. Artificial intelligence

AI features are **assistive**: they suggest and explain but **do not make compliance decisions**, do not
approve evidence and do not replace human review. When enabled, prompts and responses are recorded for
traceability and kept for up to 2 years. As of this version, the platform's generative AI features **are
not active** in production (no AI provider is configured); if they are enabled, the provider will be added
to the list in section 6 before use starts.

On the corporate website, the news section (ISO Radar) uses an AI service to draft summaries from **public
news** about standards, reviewed by a person before publication. This does not involve personal data of
visitors, subscribers or customers.

## 9. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures, including:

- centralised sign-in with open standards (OIDC), mandatory second factor for administrative accounts and a
  strong password policy;
- organisation isolation on every query, with automated tests that check for leaks between customers;
- encryption in transit (HTTPS) on every connection and encryption of backups;
- access to evidence only through temporary signed links, valid for at most 15 minutes;
- an audit trail with a verifiable integrity chain;
- invitations through a single-use activation link, without sending passwords by email;
- databases without public access and backups protected against deletion;
- logging of sign-in events and administrative actions, with operational alerts.

No system is entirely free of risk; we review these controls continuously.

## 11. Your rights

Under LGPD art. 18 (and GDPR arts. 15–22, where applicable) you may request:

- confirmation that we process your data and access to it;
- correction of incomplete, inaccurate or out-of-date data;
- anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data or data processed unlawfully;
- portability;
- information on who we share your data with;
- review of decisions taken solely by automated processing;
- objection to processing based on legitimate interest.

Send requests to **dpo@auditcockpits.com**. Confirmation that we process your data can be given in simplified
form immediately; the full answer is sent within **15 days** (LGPD art. 19). Deletion, anonymisation and
portability of account data are carried out by our team, in the database and in the identity provider, on
your request. To stop receiving the newsletter, use the link in each email. We may ask for information to
confirm your identity. If the request concerns data we process as processor for a customer company, we will
forward it to that company and let you know.

You may also complain to the **Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD)** or, where the GDPR
applies, to the data protection authority of your country.

## 12. Security incidents

If an incident occurs that may cause relevant risk or harm to individuals, we will follow our internal
containment, assessment and recording procedure and will notify the affected customer companies (within
48 hours of confirmation, when it involves data we process on their behalf), the individuals and the ANPD
within the applicable regulatory deadlines (currently 3 business days — ANPD Resolution No. 15/2024).
