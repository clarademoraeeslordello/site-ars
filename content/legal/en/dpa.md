## 1. Parties and roles

1.1. The **Customer** (identified in the commercial contract or in the sign-up record) acts as **controller** of the personal data
contained in Customer Content (LGPD art. 5(VI); GDPR art. 4(7)).

1.2. **.NETT TECNOLOGIA LTDA**, CNPJ 62.056.161/0001-54, registered office at Rua Pais Leme, 215, suite 1713, Pinheiros, São Paulo/SP, CEP 05424-150, Brazil ("Audit Cockpits"), acts as **operator**
(LGPD art. 5(VII)) / **processor** (GDPR arts. 4(8) and 28).

1.3. This DPA does not apply to data for which Audit Cockpits is controller (accounts, authentication,
security, support, billing), which is governed by the Privacy Policy.

1.4. Where the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies, this DPA is the contract required by GDPR art. 28(3).

## 2. Subject matter, nature, purpose and duration

2.1. **Subject matter:** processing of personal data needed to provide the Audit Cockpits platform.

2.2. **Nature:** hosting, storage, organisation, consultation, display, sending notifications, generating
reports, backup and deletion.

2.3. **Purpose:** solely providing the contracted service and following the Customer's instructions.

2.4. **Duration:** the term of the contract, plus the return and deletion periods of clause 13.

## 3. Documented instructions

3.1. Audit Cockpits will process personal data **only on the Customer's documented instructions**. The
contract, the Terms, this DPA and the settings and actions of the Customer's Users on the platform are
instructions.

3.2. Audit Cockpits will tell the Customer if it believes an instruction breaches the LGPD, the GDPR or other
applicable law, and may suspend it until clarified.

3.3. If the law requires different processing, Audit Cockpits will inform the Customer first, unless the law
prohibits this.

## 4. Staff confidentiality

Audit Cockpits ensures that people authorised to process the data are bound by contractual or statutory
confidentiality and only access it as needed for their duties.

## 5. Security measures

5.1. Audit Cockpits applies the technical and organisational measures described in **Annex II**, appropriate to
the risk (LGPD art. 46; GDPR art. 32).

5.2. Measures may be updated provided the overall level of protection is not reduced.

## 6. Subprocessors

6.1. The Customer gives **general authorisation** to engage the subprocessors listed in **Annex III** and on the
public [Subprocessors](legal:suboperadores) page.

6.2. Audit Cockpits will notify the Customer of a new or replaced subprocessor at least **30 days** in advance, by email to the Customer's Administrators and an update to the public page.

6.3. The Customer may **object** on reasonable grounds, in writing, within the notice period. The parties will
seek a solution in good faith; failing that, the Customer may terminate the affected part of the service
without penalty.

6.4. Audit Cockpits will impose on each subprocessor data protection obligations substantially equivalent to
this DPA and remains liable to the Customer for its acts, within the limits of the law.

## 7. Assistance with data subject rights

7.1. Requests from individuals received by Audit Cockpits about Customer Content will be **forwarded to the
Customer** without undue delay, without answering on the merits, unless the Customer instructs otherwise.

7.2. Audit Cockpits will help the Customer meet LGPD art. 18 and GDPR arts. 15–22 rights: through platform
features (consultation, correction, archiving and reports exportable as PDF and Excel) and, for a full export
or permanent deletion of data, on request to support, carried out by the Audit Cockpits team.

## 8. Security incidents

8.1. Audit Cockpits will notify the Customer of a security incident affecting personal data in Customer Content
**without undue delay and within 48 (forty-eight) hours of confirming** the incident.

8.2. The notice will include, as far as available (and supplemented in stages): the nature of the incident;
categories and approximate number of individuals and records; data affected; likely consequences; measures
taken and proposed; and the DPO's contact.

8.3. This deadline lets the Customer, as controller, notify the ANPD and individuals within **3 business days**
(ANPD Resolution No. 15/2024) and, where applicable, the supervisory authority within **72 hours** (GDPR
art. 33).

8.4. Notification by Audit Cockpits is not an admission of fault. The Customer decides on notifying the
authority and individuals, with reasonable assistance from Audit Cockpits.

## 9. DPIA assistance and prior consultation

Audit Cockpits will provide reasonable information about the service so that the Customer can prepare a data
protection impact report (LGPD art. 38) or DPIA (GDPR art. 35) and, if needed, consult the competent authority
(GDPR art. 36).

## 10. International transfers

10.1. Data is **hosted in the European Union** (Netherlands). Some subprocessors process data in the **United
States** (transactional email; weekly backup restore test, in a temporary environment discarded at the end of
the test) and on Cloudflare's global edge network (Annex III).

10.2. Transfers from Brazil follow **LGPD art. 33**, in particular standard contractual clauses and the
suppliers' contractual guarantees, in line with ANPD Resolution No. 19/2024, whose standard clauses are incorporated into subprocessor contracts within the adaptation period it sets.

10.3. Where the GDPR/UK GDPR applies and the Customer is in the EEA or the UK, sending data to Audit Cockpits
(established in Brazil) is an international transfer; it relies on the **Standard Contractual Clauses**
(Decision 2021/914), **Module 2** (controller → processor), and, for the UK, on the **UK Addendum**
(International Data Transfer Addendum). Data stays hosted in the EU. Transfers from Audit Cockpits to
subprocessors outside the EEA/UK rely on an adequacy decision (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework
where the supplier is certified) or on the European Commission's **Standard Contractual Clauses** (Decision
2021/914, Module 2 or 3 as appropriate) and, for the UK, the UK Addendum. The parties agree that, where
needed, the SCCs are incorporated by reference into this DPA, governed by the law of **the Netherlands**, with the courts of the Netherlands as forum (SCC clauses 17 and 18).

## 10A. Terms for Customers in the United States

Where a US state privacy law (such as the CCPA/CPRA) applies to the Customer, Audit Cockpits acts as a
**service provider** / **processor** and undertakes to: (a) not sell or share personal data in Customer
Content; (b) not retain, use or disclose that data for any purpose other than providing the service; (c) not
combine it with data from other sources except as the law permits; (d) comply with the applicable obligations
and notify the Customer if it can no longer meet them; and (e) allow the Customer to take reasonable steps to
stop unauthorised use.

## 11. Audits and inspections

11.1. Audit Cockpits will make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance with this DPA,
primarily through **reports, security documentation and answers to reasonable questionnaires**, up to **once** a year.

11.2. If that information is insufficient, or an authority requires it, the Customer may carry out an
inspection, itself or through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality, with **30 days'** notice, during business hours, without access to other customers' data and without
compromising platform security. The Customer bears the costs of the inspection unless it reveals a material breach of this DPA by Audit Cockpits.

## 12. Partner consultancies

12.1. If the Customer is served by a Partner Consultancy (Commercial or White label models), the partner's
consultants access the Customer's Organisation **under the Customer's authorisation** (a grant approved by
Audit Cockpits), with a limited role, a default term of 12 months and revocable by the Customer's
Administrator at any time.

12.2. With that access the Partner Consultancy acts **on behalf of the Customer**, as a processor engaged by
the Customer (not as a subprocessor of Audit Cockpits), and is **liable to the Customer** for how its
consultants use the access. Audit Cockpits is responsible for the platform controls (role limit, term,
revocation and audit trail), not for the partner's conduct.

12.3. In the Referral model, the Partner Consultancy gets no access to Customer Content.

## 13. Return and deletion

13.1. Within **30 days** after the Organisation is deactivated, the Customer may ask support
to export Customer Content in a structured format (the platform reports can be downloaded at any time before
then).

13.2. After that period, data is kept and then deleted or anonymised under the Privacy Policy: evidence and
content up to 7 years after deactivation or a shorter period set in the contract by the Customer; accounts and
memberships up to 5 years; audit trail with name and IP anonymised after 10 years; unless there is a legal
duty to keep it.

13.3. Copies in encrypted **backups** are not edited individually: they expire in their retention cycle
(deletion lock for 35 days and expiry at 40 days) and stay protected and out of active use until expiry.

13.4. On request, Audit Cockpits will confirm the deletion in writing.

## 14. Liability

14.1. Each party is liable for the damage it causes in breach of data protection law, under LGPD arts. 42–45
and GDPR art. 82.

14.2. The limitation of liability in the Terms applies, except where the law does not allow it.

## 15. Term and precedence

15.1. This DPA remains in force for as long as Audit Cockpits processes personal data on the Customer's behalf.

15.2. In case of conflict, this DPA **prevails over the Terms of Use** and the commercial contract on data
protection matters. The SCCs, when incorporated, prevail over this DPA.

## Annex II — Technical and organisational measures

- Centralised authentication on an identity provider operated by Audit Cockpits, with open standards (OIDC), a
  strong password policy (minimum 12 characters), protection against repeated attempts and **mandatory MFA for
  administrative roles**.
- **Organisation isolation** on every query, with automated tests for cross-customer leaks.
- Role-based access control; consultancies and external auditors only through authorisations with scope,
  expiry and revocation.
- **HTTPS** on every connection; databases without public access.
- **Daily encrypted backups** of the databases, with a 35-day deletion lock, expiry at 40 days and a **weekly
  automatic restore test**.
- Evidence available only through **signed links valid for at most 15 minutes**; uploads go first to a
  **quarantine** area, with the hash computed by the server before moving to the final area.
- **Audit trail** with a verifiable integrity chain.
- Invitations through a **single-use link valid for 72 hours**, no password by email.
- Logging of sign-in events and administrative actions, with operational alerts.
- Automatic guard that stops the production configuration from pointing at test environments.
- Internal incident response and data subject request procedures.
- Staff confidentiality and administrative access restricted to what is necessary.
